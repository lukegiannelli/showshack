Privacy

Plain English, no tracking pixels, no ads, nothing sold. Last updated November 28, 2026.

Short version. Without an account, everything you do stays in this browser on this device. If you sign in, a copy syncs to our database so it's on your other devices too. We never sell or share it for advertising.

Without signing in

Your stars, saved nights, passport stamps and notes, settings and the place you start from for drive times are saved in your browser only (local storage). We can't see them. Clearing your browser data, or Settings & your data → Clear everything on this phone, removes them.

If you sign in

  • Your email, used only to send your sign-in code.
  • A synced copy of the things above (stars, nights, passport, settings, ticket marks, music links you added) so they follow you between devices.
  • Friends (optional): the name you pick, a random friend code, and who you've added. Friends see your stamped venues and the shows you starred.
  • Leaderboard and ratings (both off unless you turn them on): your name, venue count and nights planned/played; your star ratings of venues (never your notes).
  • Reminders (optional): a push address for your device so we can send the notifications you switched on.
  • Shared night links: the plan you share, and the first names people type when they tap "I'm in". Anyone with the link can see both.
  • Stamp photos (optional): stored privately; only you can see them.
  • Reports you send ("Missing a show?", "Something wrong with this listing?"): the text, the listing, and your account so we can follow up.

What we collect from everyone

  • Error reports: if the app breaks, it sends the error message, the file and line, the tab you were on, your browser type and the app version. No account, no name, no location. Kept 30 days.
  • Feature counts: how many devices used a feature each day (for example "starred a show"). Counts only, no names or IDs tied to you.

Services we use

  • Supabase runs our database, sign-in and reminders (US).
  • GitHub Pages serves the app.
  • Google provides fonts, and drive times between your stops when you plan a night (the stops' locations are sent to Google). The open OSRM service is a backup for drive times.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather for a night, sent only the show's area.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles, TheAudioDB artist logos, and jsDelivr for the sign-in code library.
  • Ticket, venue and payment links (Ticketmaster, venue sites, Venmo, Cash App) open those sites; what happens there is up to them.

Removing your data

  • On this device: Settings & your data → Clear everything on this phone. Sign out first if you're signed in, or your synced copy will come back.
  • Reminders: You → Reminders → turn them off on each device.
  • Friends, leaderboard, ratings: switch them off or remove friends on the You page.
  • Your whole account: You → Account & sync → Delete my account (tap twice). It deletes your sign-in, your synced copy, profile, friends, leaderboard entry, ratings, reminders, shared night links, reports, flyer shows and stamp photos, right away. What's saved on your phone stays until you clear it in Settings.

Kids

The Shack lists events for adults, some 18+ or 21+. It isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their data.
