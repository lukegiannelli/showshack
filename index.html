How the Shack knows

—listingsdailylast refresh—Ticketmaster checks
  • 📡
    Pulled from the source, every morning

    Venue calendars and ticket sites (Ticketmaster, Gametime, TickPick, Eventbrite, Songkick, venue pages) get re-read daily. Shows that already happened drop off on their own.

  • 🏷️
    Prices wear their date

    “From $X · checked Nov 4” is the cheapest seat on that day. A “≈” price is a typical range, not a live listing.

  • 🔁
    Weekly nights get spot-checked

    Of the weekly and monthly series, a batch gets re-checked every week. Older listings carry a “call ahead” note on the card.

  • 🎟️
    The Shack doesn't sell tickets

    Tickets and Info buttons go straight to the seller or organizer. Their price, their final say.

Artist names, logos and venue names belong to their owners and are shown just to help you find shows. If something should come down, tell Luke and it will.

Plans change last-minute. Peek at the seller's page before you buy or drive. Spot something off? Tell Luke 🤙
